## 💡 Product Philosophy

> **Build less, learn faster, deliver more value.**

I believe a successful product is not defined by the number of features delivered. The goal is to identify the most valuable customer problem, build a focused solution, validate it with real users and data, and continuously improve the experience.

For platform products, I especially focus on creating **reusable capabilities instead of one-off solutions** — making teams, customers, and the overall ecosystem more efficient as the product scales.
